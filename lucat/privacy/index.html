Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the current LuCat apps for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Your cat’s everyday moments belong on your devices. Here is what LuCat processes and how you can manage it.

1. Information processed

LuCat manages cat profiles, weight entries and petting sessions. The current version does not require a LuCat account. Depending on the features you use, the app processes the following information on your devices:

Cat profiles: names, breeds and other details you enter; chosen or captured avatar images; weights and measurement dates.

Petting sessions: the associated cat, start and end times, duration, Apple Watch wrist-motion samples, and the statistics, charts and movement analysis derived from them.

Preferences: language, daily goals, reminder times, sample-data settings and other choices. Operation and sync information: local transfer status, errors and diagnostic logs used to investigate problems and avoid missing or duplicate transfers.

This information supports the features you choose. The current app does not automatically upload cat profiles or motion records to a LuCat developer server. It does not provide microphone recording, heart-rate collection, advertising tracking or cloud-based AI analysis.

Built-in sample cats and sessions are demonstrations, not actual measurements. Avoid adding unnecessary sensitive personal information to names, photos or other fields.

2. Permissions are your choice

Camera access lets you take a cat’s avatar photo. Photo access lets you choose an avatar; the system authorization you grant determines which photos are accessible, and the app uses the image you select.

Motion-related access supports wrist-motion recording and analysis on Apple Watch, with permissions requested where required by the system. Notifications provide reminders at your selected local times and may contain a cat’s name or avatar.

You can refuse access or change permissions in iOS or watchOS Settings. Refusing a permission affects its related feature; it does not automatically delete previously saved data. Notifications may appear on the lock screen, so adjust notification previews as needed.

3. Storage and device sync

Profiles, sessions and related files are primarily stored in each device’s local app storage. Paired iPhone and Apple Watch devices sync using Apple’s device-communication mechanisms. When disconnected, the watch may retain unsynced data for processing when connectivity returns.

Avatars may be converted into thumbnails for the watch or local notification attachments. Changes and deletions reach the other device when syncing completes. Previously delivered notifications or files you have exported are not automatically recalled from every location.

The current app offers no LuCat account cloud backup or developer-server sync. Operating-system backup, restore and diagnostic services are managed by Apple and your system settings. Local storage does not guarantee that every copy on a device is excluded from system backups.

4. Retention and deletion

You can view and edit relevant information, turn off reminders and use the app’s available delete or clear controls. If a paired device is offline, cross-device deletion may need to wait for reconnection.

Archiving and clearing are not the same as immediate permanent erasure. Archiving a cat hides its profile but may retain associated historical sessions. Some clearing operations move files to local isolated storage while coordinating operations across devices. One tap on Delete therefore does not guarantee immediate removal of every copy on both devices. A fixed retention period for these isolated copies is not specified in this version.

Deleting an app and offloading it while keeping its documents and data are different actions. To remove app data from a device, use the system’s Delete App action and check the paired watch, exported files and system backups separately. The developer cannot remotely access or erase local data that you have not submitted.

5. Sharing and external services

When you actively share, export or send feedback and complete the corresponding action, the selected information goes to the recipient or service you choose. Review logs, screenshots and records before sending them. Recipients handle their copies under their own policies.

The optional feedback link opens Tencent Docs. LuCat does not retain personal information or diagnostic files through that feedback channel. Tencent Docs may process visits and submissions under its own policies. Opening the link does not attach your records. The app does not upload diagnostic files to LuCat; you can choose to export them yourself.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no additional advertising or analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Loading it still sends network request information, including your IP address, to the hosting service. The URL language parameter selects a display language and contains no cat-profile data.

6. Security and younger users

The app uses the system sandbox and available file-protection mechanisms and validates data transferred between devices. No device or transfer method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device passcode and keep your operating system up to date.

LuCat currently has no accounts or social features designed specifically for children. Younger users should use it with a parent or guardian’s guidance and avoid sharing sensitive information about themselves or others in avatars, feedback or external communities.

7. Contact and changes

For questions about LuCat’s data handling, use the support email or the Tencent Docs feedback link below. Please keep passwords, identity documents and sensitive personal information out of shared documents.

Changes to this policy will be reflected in the update date on this page. Features requiring additional authorization should explain the request and obtain the applicable permissions before use.
